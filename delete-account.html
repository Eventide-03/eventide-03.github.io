Delete your Quantify account

Quantify, by qtfy · Last updated: 15 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Quantify account and its data at any time, either inside the app or by emailing us. Guest mode doesn't create an account, so guest progress is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Quantify and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. If it's been a while since you signed in, the app may ask you to sign in again first to confirm it's you.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us:

Email: rishthewizard@gmail.com
Subject: Quantify account deletion request

Please include:

  • The email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with
  • Your Quantify username, if you remember it

We'll reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account and its data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, email, profile photo link, career and preferences)
  • Your username reservation
  • Progress and statistics, streak, coins, rewards and flashcard progress
  • Mock-interview history, including every answer you typed or dictated
  • Daily-question records, resume-review history and saved lesson notes
  • In-app notifications
  • Your leaderboard entry, friendships and friend requests

What is kept

  • Duels you already played. A duel is shared with your opponent, so its result, including the username you had at the time, stays in their duel history after an in-app deletion. Ask by email and we will remove those duels too.
  • Backups and service logs. Copies held in our providers' backups and logs are removed as those cycle out, normally within 30 days.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google; see how Google uses data from apps that use its services. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.
