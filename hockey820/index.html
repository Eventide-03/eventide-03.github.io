Can you build a lineup that goes 82–0?

Six positions. Six teams dealt from eight decades, and you decide where each one plays. Real players, real statistics, real history, and one question. Or open the sandbox, pick anyone you like, and play a friend on the same phone.

Coming to the App Store and Google Play

How it works

🎲

Spin

One real team, one real decade. The draw sticks: you play the hand you are dealt.

🧭

Choose

You decide which position that team fills, from the ones it genuinely had players at. The same draw can be a gift at one spot and useless at another.

🏒

Pick

Choose a player who genuinely played for that team in that era. Their statistics are from that era, not their career best.

📋

Build

Six positions laid out on the ice: three forwards, two defencemen and a goaltender. One team fills one position, so a finished lineup spans six franchises.

📊

Simulate

A full 82-game season, played out game by game, with overtime when it is tied. Going 82–0 is rare, and that is the point.

Sandbox: no spin, no limits

🤝

Head to head

Two people, one phone. You each build a six-player lineup from anyone in history, then the two teams play a single game at a neutral rink. Settle it.

🧪

Build a team

Take whoever you want from any team in any decade, then play a full 82-game season with them and see what the lineup is really worth.

🚫

None of it counts

Sandbox games pay no coins, unlock no themes and never touch your history. A lineup of six legends going 82–0 would not prove much.

Real data, not invented numbers

📚

1950 to 2026

Eight decades of real franchises, in the eras they actually existed: the Nordiques, the Whalers, the North Stars, the Atlanta Flames.

🔍

Every season is measured

Every player card shows that player's real season line for the era he was dealt from. A few categories were not recorded in the earliest decades, and the card shows only what was.

⚖️

Judged in context

Every statistic is compared with the players of that exact era, so a 1950s season and a 2020s season are judged on equal terms.

📴

No account needed

No sign-up, no login. Works completely offline, and your history stays on your device.

Player, roster and statistical data from the NHL Stats API, the public endpoints behind nhl.com, transcribed when the app is built. All historical data is real; nothing is fabricated.
