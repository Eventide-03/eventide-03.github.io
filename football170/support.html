How to play

  • Spin. You are dealt one real franchise in one real era. All Time draws a decade, Modern a five year window from 2001 on. Draws lean towards more recent decades.
  • Choose a position. You decide where that team plays on your roster. Only the positions it genuinely had players at are offered.
  • Pick a player. Choose one player from that team and era. Their statistics are from that era, not their career best.
  • Build. Fill all nine positions: four on offence (QB, RB, WR, TE), four on defence (EDGE, LB, CB, S) and a kicker. One team fills one position, so a finished roster spans nine different franchises.
  • Simulate. A 17-game schedule is played 1,000 times, and the most common record is yours.
  • 17–0? It takes a team rating of 96.7. Going undefeated is rare. That is the point.

Frequently asked

Why can't I spin again by backing out?

Draws are sticky. Leaving a position and coming back gives you the same era team. Without this, you could reroll until you got a great one, and the whole challenge would disappear.

What is sandbox mode?

The game with the rules switched off. There is no spin and no respin budget — you pick whoever you want from any team in any decade. Two formats: Head to Head, where two people on one phone each build a roster and the teams play a single game at a neutral site, and Build a Team, where you assemble a roster and play a full 17-game season with it.

Does anything in the sandbox count?

No. Sandbox games pay no coins, unlock no themes and never appear in your history or your best run. A roster of nine all-time greats going 17–0 would not prove anything, so it is not treated as though it did.

My team was rated higher and still lost the head-to-head game.

It is one game. The result screen shows the pre-game win probability for exactly that reason — a 70% favourite still loses three times in ten. Games are played at a neutral site, so neither player gets an edge for being listed first. Hit Rematch, or play a season if you want the better roster to show.

What are respins?

A respin throws away the team you were dealt and deals another one. One short video ad adds three respins, once a run. No amount of extra watching gets you a fourth. Watching it also skips the full-screen ad after that run and the next two. Once they are spent, you play the hand you are dealt. The counter in the top-right shows how many remain, and it survives closing the app.

If you close the ad before it finishes, you are not charged: the respin is not spent and your team does not change.

Why do some players show no statistics?

Because those statistics genuinely do not exist. Season-by-season records only go back to 1999. For earlier eras, the card says so plainly and the player is rated on his real career record and honours — Hall of Fame selections, Pro Bowls, All-Pro selections, seasons and games played. We would rather show you an honest gap than invent a number.

What is the number on the player cards?

In Stats Visible, every card shows the player's overall rating out of 99, the OVR. It measures how dominant the player was against their own contemporaries, so a 1970s season and a 2020s season are judged on equal terms. Every statistic is compared to the league average for that exact season. Stats Hidden never shows it.

What is the difference between Stats Visible and Stats Hidden?

Stats Visible shows real season statistics, career honours and the overall rating on each card, ordered best-first as a starting point — the numbers are there to argue with it. Stats Hidden shows only name, position, team and era, and the list is alphabetical so its order gives nothing away. The mode is fixed once a run starts.

What is the Salary Cap?

An optional rule for Stats Visible runs, switched on from the New Run screen. Every player has a cap hit set by overall rating: a 99 costs $40M, and every five points lower halves the price. All nine picks have to fit under $160M, so the top card is not always the best pick, and players you cannot afford are greyed out. Each open position keeps $1M back, so a capped roster can always be finished.

Nobody can fit a team of 99s under the cap, so a capped run is read against a record table 4.1 points lower: 17–0 takes 92.6 instead of 96.7.

I picked great players and still lost. Is it broken?

No. Your record comes from your team rating, the average of your nine players' overall ratings, read against the table in How to Play: 17–0 takes 96.7, 16–1 takes 95.2 and 15–2 takes 94.0. Each run plays the season 1,000 times and keeps the most common record, so you get the record your roster was built for, not an unlucky week. One or two weaker picks can leave the average just short of a line.

Coins and themes

How do I earn coins?

By finishing runs. Every completed run pays 10 coins, plus 1 for every win and 50 more for a perfect season. An optional short video on the results screen is worth 10 more, once a run, and opening the game on a new day adds 10.

What are they for?

Themes. Five of the eleven are bought with coins. Nothing in Football 17-0 costs real money, and there is no way to buy coins.

How do I unlock the other themes?

By playing well. There is one for a ten-win season, one for twelve, one for fifteen, one for sixteen, and one that only exists if you go 17–0. They unlock off your best run ever, so a bad season afterwards never takes one away.

Ads

Where do they appear?

  • A banner on the results screen.
  • A full-screen ad when you leave the results screen, after every second season. Watching a respin video skips it for that run and the next two.
  • A video ad when you choose to buy a respin — this one only ever plays because you asked for it.

Ads never interrupt a spin, a pick, or the moment your season is revealed — you always see your record first.

Can I pay to remove them?

Not at the moment. There is no in-app purchase or subscription in Football 17-0.

Can I stop personalised ads?

Yes, and you can still play the whole game.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off the toggle for Football 17-0.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can opt out of ad personalisation or delete your Advertising ID.
  • EU, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for consent before ads load. You can decline, and change your answer later at Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

Turning personalisation off does not remove ads or reduce how many you see — it changes how they are chosen.

An ad is frozen, or won't close.

Force-quit the app and reopen it. Your run is saved continuously and will resume where you left off, so you will not lose progress. If a particular ad does this repeatedly, send us a screenshot — the advertiser is usually identifiable from it and we can have it blocked.

Troubleshooting

My history disappeared.

History is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, and because there is no account there is no cloud copy to restore. Moving to a new device also starts you fresh.

The app is stuck mid-run.

Force-quit and reopen. Your run is saved continuously, including your draws and your remaining respins, and it will resume where you left off.

Text is too small / I use VoiceOver.

The app honours your system text size and is built for screen readers. Wins and losses are never signalled by colour alone. If you hit something that does not work with assistive technology, please tell us — we treat that as a bug.
