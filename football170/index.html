Can you build a team that goes 17–0?

Nine positions. Nine teams dealt from seven decades, and you decide where each one plays. Real players, real statistics, real history — and one question. Or open the sandbox, pick anyone you like, and play a friend on the same phone.

Coming to the App Store

How it works

🎲

Spin

One real team, one real decade. The draw sticks — you play the hand you are dealt.

🧭

Choose

You decide which position that team fills, from the ones it genuinely had players at. The same draw can be a gift at one spot and useless at another.

🏈

Pick

Choose a player who genuinely played for that team in that era. Their stats are from that era, not their career best.

📋

Build

Nine positions laid out on the field — four on offense, four on defense and a kicker. One team fills one position, so a finished roster spans nine franchises.

📊

Simulate

A full 17-game season, played out game by game. Going undefeated is rare, and that is the point.

Sandbox — no spin, no limits

🤝

Head to head

Two people, one phone. You each build a nine-player roster from anyone in history, then the two teams play a single game at a neutral site. Settle it.

🧪

Build a team

Take whoever you want from any team in any decade, then play a full 17-game season with them and see what the roster is really worth.

🚫

None of it counts

Sandbox games pay no coins, unlock no themes and never touch your history. A roster of nine legends going 17–0 would not prove much.

Real data, not invented numbers

📚

1960 to 2026

Seven decades of real franchises, in the eras they actually existed — Baltimore Colts in the sixties, Houston Oilers in the seventies.

🔍

Honest gaps

Season statistics only go back to 1999. Earlier cards say so plainly and rate the player on his real career record and honours.

⚖️

Judged in context

Every statistic is compared to the league average for that exact season, so a 1970s season and a 2020s season are judged on equal terms.

📴

No account needed

No sign-up, no login. Works completely offline, and your history stays on your device.
