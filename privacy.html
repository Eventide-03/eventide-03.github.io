Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Quantify ("we", "us") is an interview-preparation app for finance and related careers. It provides a personalized learning path, an interview question bank, flashcards, AI-scored mock interviews, an on-device resume review, a friends leaderboard, and progress tracking such as streaks and statistics. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

The short version. Quantify is free and supported by ads. Your account data (profile, progress, streaks) is used to run the app — never sold. Resume analysis happens entirely on your device and your resume is never uploaded. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier; you choose whether to allow that when iOS asks, and declining only makes ads less relevant. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Account identity. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and (from Google) profile photo URL. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case we only ever see Apple's private relay address.
  • Username. A unique public handle you choose, so friends can send you requests. It is visible to other users on the leaderboard and in friend requests.
  • Profile preferences. Target career, target role, firms of interest, school, difficulty level, and reminder settings.
  • Content you enter. Answers you type or dictate in mock interviews and the daily question, resume text you paste or scan, and notes you highlight in lessons.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Usage and progress. Questions answered, scores, sessions completed, streaks, coins earned and spent, lessons read, and bookmarks.
  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA). Only if you grant permission when iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See Advertising.
  • Push token. If you enable reminders, so we can deliver streak notifications.

c. Guest mode

You can use Quantify without an account by tapping Continue as guest. In guest mode your progress is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers. Ads still appear, since the app is ad-supported.

d. What we do not collect

We do not collect precise or coarse location, your contacts, health or fitness data, or payment card or bank details. Quantify contains no in-app purchases, so no payment information is involved at all. Resume photos you scan are read on your device and are never uploaded.

2. On-device processing

Two features run entirely locally and send nothing to a server:

  • Resume review. Scanning uses on-device text recognition, and scoring and rewrite suggestions are computed on your device. Only the resulting score and feedback are saved to your account history — never the source photo.
  • Answer scoring. Mock-interview and daily-question answers are evaluated on-device against each question's rubric.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide and personalize the app — tailoring questions, lessons and daily content to your career and difficulty;
  • Save your progress and sync it across your devices when you are signed in;
  • Operate social features — friend requests by username, duels, and the leaderboard;
  • Send streak reminders and achievement notifications if you enable them;
  • Serve ads, which fund the app being free (see below);
  • Diagnose crashes and errors, and keep the service secure.

4. Advertising

Quantify is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display banner ads, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch in exchange for in-app coins.

  • AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and ad-interaction data to select and measure ads, including personalized ads.
  • On iOS we show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before that identifier is used. If you decline — or you never respond — ads still appear but are not personalized. Nothing else in the app changes.
  • You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policy: Google Business Data Privacy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app coins have no cash value, cannot be purchased, and are not a financial product — they are earned by learning or by watching rewarded ads.

5. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with data brokers. Information is shared only with:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database and push notifications, acting as our processor to store your account and progress.
  • Google AdMob — for advertising, as described above.
  • Apple — if you use Sign in with Apple, for authentication only.
  • Other users — your username and public stats (coins, streak, duel wins) appear on the leaderboard and to friends. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Legal — where required by law, or to protect our rights or users' safety.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. If you delete your account, your profile, progress, stats, notes, resume-review history, coins and username reservation are removed from our database. Backups and provider logs may persist for a short period before cycling out.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and data — in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This is permanent and requires no email to us.
  • Sign out — Profile → Settings → Sign out.
  • Ad tracking — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Notifications — turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction and export — depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, export or restrict processing of your data. Email us and we will help.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

8. Children's privacy

Quantify is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS, and access to stored data is restricted so that each account can only read and write its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. International users

We operate using Google Cloud infrastructure and your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate. By using Quantify you consent to that transfer.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
