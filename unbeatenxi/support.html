How to play

  • Spin. You are dealt one real team in one real decade. Draws lean towards more recent decades, so you will see the 2000s to 2020s more often than the 1950s.
  • Choose a position. You decide where that team plays in your XI. Only the positions it genuinely had players at are offered.
  • Pick a player. Choose one player from that team and era. Their figures are from that era, not their career best.
  • Build. Fill all eleven positions: a goalkeeper, a back four, three in midfield and a front three. One team fills one position, so a finished XI spans eleven different teams.
  • Simulate. A 38-match league season is generated and played out. A draw is a result.
  • Unbeaten? Going a season without a defeat is rare. That is the point.

Frequently asked

Why can't I spin again by backing out?

Draws are sticky. Leaving the draft and coming back gives you the same team. Without this, you could reroll until you got a great one, and the whole challenge would disappear.

What if the team I'm dealt has nobody at the position I need?

Then that position is not offered, and it stays open for a later draw. The game only ever shows you positions the drawn team genuinely had players at, so you will never open an empty list. If a draw is useless to you, that is what respins are for.

What are respins?

A respin throws away the team you were dealt and deals another one. Respins come from a short video ad: one video adds two respins, up to four per run. No amount of extra watching gets you a fifth. Once they are spent, you play the hand you are dealt. The counter in the top-right shows how many remain, and it survives closing the app.

If you close the ad before it finishes, or no ad was available, nothing is charged: the respin is not spent and your team does not change.

Where do the statistics on the cards come from?

Every card shows the player's real career line for the spell he was dealt from: appearances, goals and years at that club, or caps and goals for a national team. A few players have no figures because the public record holds none for them. Their cards say so plainly, and they are rated on what is known. Career honours are real too, from the Ballon d'Or and the Golden Shoe to the World Cup Golden Ball.

Why is there no overall rating on the player cards?

Because a single number on each card turns every pick into "take the biggest one", and the actual football becomes decoration. Players do have a hidden rating that drives the simulation. It measures how dominant a player was against his own contemporaries, so a 1950s season and a 2020s season are judged on equal terms.

What is the difference between Stats Visible and Stats Hidden?

Stats Visible shows real figures and career honours on each card, ordered best-first as a starting point, with the numbers there to argue with. Stats Hidden shows only name, position, team and era, and the list is alphabetical so its order gives nothing away. How well do you know your football history? The mode is fixed once a run starts.

I picked great players and still lost. Is it broken?

No. A strong XI wins more often, but the simulation includes genuine variance from one match to the next, the same way real seasons do. Going unbeaten is meant to be hard. Check your XI rating on the results screen: if it is high and you still lost three, you were unlucky, and that is the game working as intended.

Sandbox

What is it?

The game with the rules switched off. There is no spin and no respin budget: you pick whoever you want from any team in any decade. Two formats. Head to Head, where two people on one phone each build an XI and the two teams play a single match at a neutral venue, where a draw is a result. The second player does not see the first player's picks. And Build a Team, where you assemble whoever you like and play a full 38-match season with them.

Does anything in the sandbox count?

No. Sandbox games pay no coins, unlock no themes and never appear in your history or your best run.

My team was rated higher and still lost the head-to-head match.

It is one match. The result screen shows the pre-match win probability for exactly that reason: a 70% favourite still loses three times in ten. Neither side gets an edge for being listed first. Hit Rematch, or play a season if you want the better XI to show.

Can I change a pick?

Any of them, any time, right up until the match is played. Tap a position that is already filled and pick somebody else. Changing a XI clears any result it already produced.

Coins and themes

How do I earn coins?

By finishing runs. Every completed run pays 10 coins, plus about one for every two wins, 25 more for an unbeaten season and 50 for winning every match. Coins are counted from the runs in your history, so clearing the history resets the balance. Themes you had already bought stay yours.

What are they for?

Themes. Five of the eleven are bought with coins. Nothing in Football and Soccer XI costs real money, and there is no way to buy coins.

How do I unlock the other themes?

By playing well. There is one for a 20-win season, one for 25, one for 30, one for going unbeaten, and one that only exists if you win all 38. They unlock off your best run ever, so a bad season afterwards never takes one away.

Ads

Where do they appear?

  • A banner on the results screen.
  • A full-screen ad when you leave the results screen after finishing a season.
  • A video ad when you choose to buy respins. This one only ever plays because you asked for it.

Ads never interrupt a spin, a pick, or the moment your season is revealed. You always see your record first.

Can I pay to remove them?

Not at the moment. There is no in-app purchase or subscription in Football and Soccer XI.

Can I stop personalised ads?

Yes, and you can still play the whole game.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off the toggle for Football and Soccer XI.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can opt out of ad personalisation or delete your Advertising ID.
  • EU, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for consent before ads load. You can decline, and change your answer later at Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

Turning personalisation off does not remove ads or reduce how many you see. It changes how they are chosen.

An ad is frozen, or won't close.

Force-quit the app and reopen it. Your run is saved continuously and will resume where you left off, so you will not lose progress. If a particular ad does this repeatedly, send us a screenshot. The advertiser is usually identifiable from it and we can have it blocked.

Troubleshooting

My history disappeared.

History is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, and because there is no account there is no cloud copy to restore. Moving to a new device also starts you fresh.

The app is stuck mid-run.

Force-quit and reopen. Your run is saved continuously, including your draws and your remaining respins, and it will resume where you left off.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to load ads. The game itself, spinning, picking, simulating and your history, works entirely offline.

Text is too small / I use a screen reader.

The app honours your system text size and is built for screen readers. Wins and losses are never signalled by colour alone. If you hit something that does not work with assistive technology, please tell us. We treat that as a bug.

About the data

Player careers, appearances and goals come from English Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Honours and team names in other languages come from Wikidata (CC0). All historical data is real; nothing is fabricated.
