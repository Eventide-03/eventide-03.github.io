Can you build an XI that goes a season unbeaten?

Eleven positions. Eleven teams dealt from eight decades, and you decide where each one plays. Real players, real careers, real history, and one question. Or open the sandbox, pick anyone you like, and play a friend on the same phone.

Coming to the App Store and Google Play

How it works

🎲

Spin

One real team, one real decade. The draw sticks: you play the hand you are dealt.

🧭

Choose

You decide which position that team fills, from the ones it genuinely had players at. The same draw can be a gift at one spot and useless at another.

⚽

Pick

Choose a player who genuinely played for that team in that era. Their appearances, goals and years are from that spell, not a career total.

📋

Build

Eleven positions in a 4-3-3: a goalkeeper, a back four, three in midfield and a front three. One team fills one position, so a finished XI spans eleven teams.

📊

Simulate

A full 38-match league season, played out match by match. Draws stand. Going a whole season unbeaten is rare, and that is the point.

🌍

In your language

The whole game in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Polish, Russian and Chinese, with team names in each.

Sandbox: no spin, no limits

🤝

Head to head

Two people, one phone. You each build an XI from anyone in history, then the two teams play a single match at a neutral venue. A draw is a result. Settle it.

🧪

Build a team

Take whoever you want from any team in any decade, then play a full 38-match season with them and see what the XI is really worth.

🚫

None of it counts

Sandbox games pay no coins, unlock no themes and never touch your history. An XI of eleven legends going unbeaten would not prove much.

Real data, not invented numbers

📚

1950 to 2026

Eight decades of real clubs and national teams, from the biggest leagues in Europe and South America to the sides that won the World Cup.

🔍

Honest gaps

A few players have no appearance or goal figures because the public record holds none. Their cards say so plainly rather than show a made-up number.

⚖️

Judged in context

Every statistic is compared with the players of that exact era, so a 1950s season and a 2020s season are judged on equal terms.

📴

No account needed

No sign-up, no login. Works completely offline, and your history stays on your device.
