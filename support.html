Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue as guest to use the app without signing in — your progress is saved on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google if you'd like your progress synced to your account and access to friends and the leaderboard.

Does Quantify cost anything?

No. Quantify is completely free with no in-app purchases and no subscription. It's supported by ads, and you earn coins by learning — answering the daily question, finishing lessons, passing chapter tests and winning duels — or by watching an optional rewarded ad. Coins unlock extra mock interviews and resume reviews. Coins can't be bought and have no cash value.

How do the free interviews and resume reviews work?

Your first mock interview each day is free; additional ones cost coins. You also get one free resume review when you first install the app; after that each review costs coins.

How do I change my career or difficulty?

Profile → Settings. You can change your target career and daily difficulty at any time.

Is my resume private?

Yes. Scanning and scoring run on your device — your resume photo is never uploaded. Only the resulting score and feedback are saved to your history.

How do I add a friend?

Open Profile → Friends & Duels and tap the add-friend icon, then enter their exact username. They'll get a request to accept. Both of you need to be signed in (friends aren't available in guest mode), and usernames aren't case-sensitive.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. When you first open the app iOS asks whether Quantify may track you. Decline and ads become non-personalized — everything else works the same. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, progress, stats, notes, resume history, coins and username — no email to us required. To just sign out, use Profile → Settings → Sign out.

The app says it can't connect.

Quantify works offline — tap Continue offline if prompted and your changes sync when you're back online. If problems persist, check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and reopen the app.
